# Meraki Community Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive community, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity.

Be considerate, accept constructive feedback, explain disagreements with evidence and respect different experiences. Harassment, sexualized conduct, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

This policy applies to project spaces and public representation of the project. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, issue warnings, restrict participation or impose temporary or permanent bans. Decisions should be proportional, explained when appropriate and protect the privacy of affected people. Repeated or severe violations may result in immediate removal.

Report concerns privately through the repository's Security tab (Report a vulnerability), marking the report as a community conduct concern. Do not post sensitive allegations or personal information in public issues. Maintainers must enable private reporting before community launch. Reports will be reviewed by Meraki Core Team; a maintainer involved in a report must recuse themselves. Appeals may be submitted through the same private channel for review by an uninvolved maintainer.
